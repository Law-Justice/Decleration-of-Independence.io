The Clerk’s Journal

The Committee of Five and the Declaration of Independence: an interactive graphic novel

You are a young clerk in Philadelphia in 1776. As the Declaration of Independence is proposed, drafted, argued over, and adopted, you keep a journal: you read the story, study the sources, answer questions, and put the pages together yourself.

  1. Read each comic page and turn it with Next or the arrow keys.
  2. Clip quotes, lens notes, and dated clippings to your scrapbook.
  3. Write your analysis of the Preamble, explain three to five of the charges against King George III, and finish with a reflection.
  4. Publish a front page for every part of the story, pinning your best finds.
  5. Download your essay as a Word document and keep your finished book.

Back to the assignment options. You only need to complete one option.

Quotes are real and labeled by source. The art is stylized and the narration is the game’s own. Your work saves in this browser.
